    text: |-
      I was on a team with four 2D and 3D animators. As the VFX artist I knew I wouldn’t help much with the animation itself, so I took on the rest of the pipeline. We built the story together, then I cut our storyboards into a timed animatic in Premiere to check our scope.

      While everyone animated, I gathered backgrounds and effects for the comp and pulled sounds to start the sound design. As finished shots came in, I composited each one in After Effects: layering, backgrounds, effects, and some subtle retiming. Then I did the final edit in Premiere.
