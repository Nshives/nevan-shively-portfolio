    text: |-
      I was heading off to college with nothing but a simple mason-jar coffee filter. Over the summer, using a slow-drip machine, I’d learned that drip coffee can taste really good if the water goes on slowly and evenly. So I decided to build my own machine that does that.

      I brainstormed designs and made a few drawings, then pitched the idea to Claude, made some changes, and worked with Claude to build the CAD model. Once I was happy with it I ordered the parts and 3D printed the rest. The prints didn’t come out perfectly, so I drilled some holes by hand and used extra glue to make everything fit. I use it pretty much every day.
