    text: |-
      My first idea mixed fighting waves of enemies with rowing sections. It was too much for the deadline, and I leaned on AI more than I wanted while trying to save it, so I pivoted to a version I could build myself. I built it in plain shapes first, a white box arriving on a drum hit, then the ring, and only made the art once it felt right.

      Timing was the core problem. Each enemy is given the beat it has to be hit on and works backward: it spawns three beats early out of its key’s wedge, and its position comes from the song’s clock, not the frame count. A press within 45 ms is Perfect, up to 140 ms is Good, and the rings around Orpheus are drawn exactly as thick as those windows.
