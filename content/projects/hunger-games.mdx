    text: |-
      This was a massive project. My sister and I rewrote the trailer’s audio, changing lines to fit the new concept. I broke the trailer into every shot, around 85, and built a spreadsheet of who was needed, where to shoot, what equipment I needed and when. Then we filmed.

      In the edit I cut to the original’s exact timing. A few shots were filmed on green screen and composited over clean plates I made from the original. The titles were their own challenge: a parody of the Lionsgate logo and the 3D title at the start and end, both made in Blender, plus stylized text cards between scenes, made in After Effects, that look almost identical to the original.
