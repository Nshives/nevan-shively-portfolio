    text: |-
      I started from a set of dramatically lit portraits of myself. The sketch samples the color of each cell in a grid over the photo, then draws a box of that color at that spot, in a shuffled order and at random sizes. A second pass adds cross-hatching whose density follows each spot’s brightness. Last, I drew hand-placed lines on top with Processing shapes, finding their coordinates in Photoshop.

      I typed all the code and planned almost all the logic. AI helped me debug and gave me a few pieces: the shuffle, the mask that limits the hatching, and the brightness formula. The hatching didn’t give quite the effect I wanted, but I understand every line of it.
