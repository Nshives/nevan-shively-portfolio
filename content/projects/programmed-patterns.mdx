      I started with optical illusions and stained glass, but the illusions felt like copying and stained glass didn’t translate digitally. Recreating the paper project in code felt like the right challenge.

      I mapped the shape’s six parts on paper and aimed for a 10 × 10 grid, but hand-entering 100 tiles was far too tedious, so I pivoted to 5 × 5. I didn’t lock the fills and rotations up front. I kept adjusting each tile until the whole grid felt right.
